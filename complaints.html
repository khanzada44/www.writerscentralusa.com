Complaint & FAQ

Writers Central USA Support Center

At Writers Central USA, we understand that authors may sometimes have concerns, complaints, or questions regarding our self-publishing services, writing services, book formatting, proofreading, book cover design, or publishing process. We take every complaint seriously and treat all feedback as an opportunity to improve our services, communication, and author experience.

This page is designed to help clients:

  • Submit a complaint
  • Understand common concerns
  • Get clarity on service processes
  • Learn how we resolve issues
  • Contact our support team directly

Complaints & Support Information

If you have a complaint regarding delay, communication, publishing, writing, formatting, or service experience, please know we are actively working to improve response time and support systems. At times, due to high demand for self-publishing services, writing services, and book cover design projects, there may be delays in calls or replies. We acknowledge this and have taken corrective steps.

Click the button below to view detailed breakdown and resolution steps:

We serve a growing number of indie authors, first-time writers, and publishing clients, and during peak periods, we may experience:

  • Delayed call responses
  • Late WhatsApp or email replies
  • Scheduling delays for consultations
  • Extended turnaround times for writing or editing

We understand how important timing is for authors working on Amazon publishing, book launches, and marketing deadlines.

To handle complaints and improve service quality, we have upgraded our systems:

  • 1. Centralized Communication System (CMS): We now use a structured CMS to manage all client complaints, writing requests, and publishing support queries efficiently.
  • 2. AI-Assisted Support System: We have implemented AI tools to help handle basic writing inquiries, complaint routing, and response prioritization, ensuring faster first replies.
  • 3. Improved Team Shifts & Availability: Our support team now works in structured shifts to provide better coverage across different time zones, including evening and off-peak hours.
  • 4. Better Call Handling System: We are improving our call support structure so clients can reach us more reliably for publishing, writing, and formatting assistance.

If you have a complaint about any of our services, here is how it is handled:

  • Step 1: Complaint Review - We carefully review your complaint related to writing services, editing, formatting, publishing, or communication delays.
  • Step 2: Internal Investigation - Our team checks project status, communication logs, and service records.
  • Step 3: Response & Resolution - We respond with clarification, correction, or resolution depending on the case.
  • Step 4: Improvement Action - We use every complaint to improve our self-publishing workflow, writing services, and author support systems.
